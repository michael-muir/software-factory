## Goal

The live run on #82 (job `e31-evidence-tags`, issue #105) found that the driver counted the Verifier's session as done because it exited 0, though its own gate went red. Now the review stage needs a green driver gate report for the head, and a red or missing `VERDICT:` from the Verifier stops the job.

## Acceptance criteria

- E13 AC-13 (amended 26 Nov 2026): a Verifier whose reply's last `VERDICT:` line is `VERDICT: fail <checks>` stops the job as `needs-human` with its reason, even when its session exits 0, and no later reviewer runs.
- E13 AC-13: a Verifier whose last `VERDICT:` line is `VERDICT: pass` lets the five reviewers run as before.
- E13 AC-13: a Verifier reply with no `VERDICT:` line, or a log with no readable `result`, stops the job.
- E13 AC-13: the review stage won't start, and `check review` fails, unless the driver's newest gate report for the task is a pass for the head, or for a commit whose only later changes are to `.agentic/tasks/<task>.md`.

## Notes

- The product owner's decisions of 26 Nov 2026: trust both sources; the verdict is the last `VERDICT:` line of the session's JSON `result` (with H2's #106 that reply is also the Verifier's handoff note); a red verdict stops the job and doesn't go back to the gate stage.
