## Goal

The live run on #82 (issue #106) found that the Verifier and the Spec Reviewer couldn't record a handoff or commit, and that each reran the full gate, which takes about 60 minutes on the PO's Windows machine. Now a reviewer without a shell ends its session with its handoff note as its final reply, and the driver records and commits it. Every review session gets the driver's newest gate report, and the Verifier checks that report instead of running the gate.

## Acceptance criteria

- E13 AC-07 (amended 26 Nov 2026): the prompt for a role without a shell ends with the line "Don't commit: the driver commits your files. End with your handoff note as your final reply, and stop." A role with a shell keeps "Commit your work and stop."
- E13 AC-13 (amended 26 Nov 2026): the driver hands each of the six review sessions the task's newest gate report as `$FACTORY_GATE_REPORT`, and the prompt names it. The Verifier's prompt says to check the report and not to run the gate. The others' says they may run `gate.py --checks <name>`, never the full gate.
- E13 AC-13 (amended 26 Nov 2026): after the session of a reviewer without a shell, the driver takes the reply (the JSON `result` field, or plain stdout without JSON), records it intact with `state.py handoff <task> --from <role> --body-file <scratch>`, and commits the note with the task file's findings. A reply without the three handoff headings adds a warning to the record, and the stage carries on.
