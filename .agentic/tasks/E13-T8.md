## Goal

The live run on #82 (#109) found that no pipeline role can relock a locked test, that `agents.py` had no way to record who approved a relock, and that `pipeline.py approve` knew no relock. A Debugger's "needs a human" was prose only, because `claude -p` exits 0 whatever the agent decides, so the driver spent its gate retries anyway. The product owner's decisions of 26 Nov 2026 (ADR 0047): `agents.py lock --approved-by NAME` comes to main from `dc006d3`; `pipeline.py approve <job> relock --task <id> --by NAME [--files f...]` records a relock that the next `run` carries out; and a Debugger stops the job by recording an open blocker finding.

## Acceptance criteria

- E03 AC-09 (amended 26 Nov 2026): `agents.py lock --approved-by NAME` writes `approved_by` into the lock record, and refuses a blank name. `agents.py check` accepts `approved_by` as an optional non-empty string. A record without it stays valid.
- E13 AC-12 (amended 26 Nov 2026): after each Debugger session in the gate stage, a new open blocker finding in the task file stops the job as `needs-human`, lists the findings, and spends no more gate retries.
- E13 AC-23 (new, 26 Nov 2026): `pipeline.py approve <job> relock --task <id> --by NAME [--files f...]` records the decision with its files while the job is `needs-human`, and sets it `ready`. The next `run` relocks with `agents.py lock --replace <task> <every locked file, plus the approved ones> --approved-by NAME` on the slice's runner job, after checking that only the approved files' hashes changed, commits `test(<task>): relock approved by NAME`, and reruns the stopped stage.

## Notes

- `agents.py` is brought over from `dc006d3` byte for byte, in the kit and this repo's install. `--approved-by` stays optional. E08-T2 is not relocked here.
- The relock commit also marks the Debugger's blocker findings that stopped the job as fixed: the human's decision answers them, so the review stage doesn't stop on them again.
