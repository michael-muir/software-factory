- `.factory/runner/run.py`, standard library only:
  - `submit`, `send`, `close`, `status`, `usage`, `cancel` and `stop` commands, over a file queue under the git common dir. Every change is atomic, with no locks (ADR 0030).
  - `--daemon` runs queued jobs, at most `max_parallel` at once, each in its own worktree at `../<repo>.worktrees/<id>` on branch `factory/<id>`. A worktree is removed on success and kept on a halt or failure.

  - The turn, cost and time caps halt a job (ADR 0032). This is #16's done-when, tested on real daemons on all three OSes.
